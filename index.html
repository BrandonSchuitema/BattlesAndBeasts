Learn about the game and how to play it on this website.

Overview

Battles and Beasts is the brain child of the games creator: Michael. He got sick and tired of other table top games being completely unsatisfying to play, so he created his own. The game is designed to be played with your friends (or close enemies) on paper in person.

Online games are fun and all, but Michael really wanted to bring back that in-person experience. Having a group of friends come over to play a game makes the entire experience worth more than any game could give.

This website comes with several useful tools and features that allow you to be enjoy your playing experience.

I know that as you're reading this, your mouth is already salivating at the thought of playing. So, don't be afraid to read the rules, and start playing.

The game is still fresh and raw from creation, so there may be some problems (Michael is strong enough to admit that), so don't be alarmed if there's changes to the game in the future. You can read all about those changes though in the Updates section of the website.

You better enjoy this game or else! That's a joke, not a threat. Don't sue me.
